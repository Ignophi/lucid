# Privacy

Lucid collects nothing and sends nothing.

- **No account, ads, analytics or tracking.** Lucid has no servers and does
  not share data with anyone.
- **Your data stays on your phone**: sessions, ratings, comments, settings and
  measurements are stored in the app. Automatic backups are saved to
  `Download/Lucid` on your phone; exports go wherever you choose.
- **Motion sensors** (accelerometer) are read only during a session, if
  Measurements > Motion is on, and turned into numbers such as stillness and
  estimated breaths.
- **Microphone**: only if you turn on Measurements > Sound (off by default),
  and only during a session. Sound is turned into loudness numbers as it comes
  in; no audio is recorded or stored.
- **Notifications** are used for reminders (if you turn them on) and to show a
  session in progress.

Uninstalling Lucid deletes its data from the app; backups in your Download
folder stay until you delete them.
